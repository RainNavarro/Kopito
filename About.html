How It Started

A small counter with a big habit of welcome.

KoPito began as a weekend pop-up serving hand-pulled espresso, pastries, and cold drinks to friends after class and work. What stayed with us was not only the coffee, but the rhythm around it: people pausing, talking longer, and coming back with someone new.

Today, the store keeps that same spirit. Every drink is made to feel personal, every pastry is chosen to sit well beside coffee, and every table is meant to hold a little extra time.
